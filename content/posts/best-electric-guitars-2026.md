The "best" electric guitar is the one that fits your music and your hands. These models cover the main styles, from beginner-friendly classics to stage-ready instruments. Lineups and prices change each year, so check the current version.

## Key choices

- **Single-coil pickups:** bright, clear and glassy. Great for funk, blues, indie and country.
- **Humbuckers:** thicker, louder and quieter. Great for rock and metal.
- **Fixed bridge vs. tremolo:** fixed bridges stay in tune more easily; tremolos add vibrato effects.
- **Scale length:** shorter scales (like Gibson-style) feel slinkier; longer scales (Fender-style) sound tighter.

## Our picks

### Squier Stratocaster (Bullet / Classic Vibe)
![Squier Stratocaster](/uploads/2023/01/Fender-Squier-Electric-Guitar-1.jpg)
The classic beginner Strat. Versatile, comfortable and easy to upgrade. **Best for:** first electric guitars.

### Yamaha Pacifica
![Yamaha Pacifica](/uploads/2023/01/Yamaha-Pacifica-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
Often named one of the best-value electrics ever made, with single-coil and humbucker sounds in one guitar. **Best for:** beginners who want versatility.

### Epiphone Les Paul Standard
![Epiphone Les Paul](/uploads/2023/01/Epiphone-Les-Paul-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
Classic Les Paul looks and humbucker tone at a fraction of the Gibson price. **Best for:** classic rock and blues.

### Yamaha Revstar
![Yamaha Revstar](/uploads/2023/01/Yamaha-Revstar-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
A modern, well-built design inspired by café-racer motorcycles, with a punchy voice. **Best for:** players who want something a little different.

### Fender Jazzmaster
![Fender Jazzmaster](/uploads/2023/01/Fender-Jazzmaster-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
Offset body, wide pickups and a shimmery, surf-to-shoegaze sound. **Best for:** indie and alternative players.

### Gretsch Electromatic
![Gretsch Electromatic](/uploads/2023/01/Gretsche-Electromatic-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
Hollow and semi-hollow bodies with a twangy, vintage character. **Best for:** rockabilly, country and roots music.

### Reverend Double Agent
![Reverend Double Agent](/uploads/2023/01/Reverend-Double-Agent-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
A P90-style neck pickup and a humbucker at the bridge give a wide range of tones. **Best for:** players who want one guitar for many sounds.

### Schecter Hellraiser
![Schecter Hellraiser](/uploads/2023/01/Schecter-Hellraiser-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
High-output pickups and a fast neck built for heavy music. **Best for:** metal.

### ESP LTD
![ESP LTD electric guitar](/uploads/2023/01/ESP-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
ESP's LTD line brings pro-style features to more affordable prices. **Best for:** hard rock and metal on a budget.

### Charvel Pro-Mod
![Charvel Pro-Mod](/uploads/2023/01/Charvel-Pro-Mod-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
Fast necks, hot pickups and locking tremolos on many models. **Best for:** shred and lead playing.
