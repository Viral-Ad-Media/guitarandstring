A good acoustic guitar is easy to play, stays in tune and sounds better the more you play it. These are long-standing models and model families with strong reputations. Specs and prices change between years, so check the current version before buying.

## What matters most

- **Solid top vs. laminate:** a solid wood top usually sounds richer and improves with age. Laminate is tougher and cheaper.
- **Body shape:** dreadnoughts are loud and bassy, grand auditorium and concert bodies are more balanced and comfortable.
- **Electronics:** an acoustic-electric lets you plug into an amp or PA.
- **Playability:** a proper setup matters as much as the brand.

## Our picks

### Yamaha FG / FS series
![Yamaha acoustic guitar](/uploads/2023/01/Yamaha-Acoustic-Guitar.jpg)
Yamaha's entry solid-top models are famous for value and consistent build quality. **Best for:** beginners who want a guitar they won't outgrow quickly.

### Seagull S6 Original
![Seagull S6 cedar acoustic guitar](/uploads/2023/01/Seagull-Cedar-Acoustic-Guitar.jpg)
Made in Canada with a solid cedar top that gives a warm, open sound, and a slightly narrower headstock design that helps tuning stability. **Best for:** fingerstyle and folk players on a budget.

### Taylor Grand Auditorium (100–200 series)
![Taylor Grand Auditorium acoustic guitar](/uploads/2023/01/Taylor-Grand-Auditorium-Acoustic-Guitar.jpg)
Taylor necks are known for being very easy to play, and the Grand Auditorium shape is balanced for both strumming and picking. **Best for:** players upgrading from a first guitar.

### Martin Standard Series
![Martin acoustic guitar](/uploads/2023/01/Martin-Acoustic-Guitar.jpg)
Martin dreadnoughts like the D-28 defined the modern acoustic sound: deep bass, strong projection and a classic look. **Best for:** players buying a lifetime instrument.

### Takamine G-Series Dreadnought
![Takamine dreadnought acoustic guitar](/uploads/2023/01/Takamine-Dreadnought-Acoustic-Guitar.jpg)
Takamine is respected for stage-ready acoustic-electrics with reliable pickups. **Best for:** gigging on a budget.

### Ibanez AW / AC series
![Ibanez acoustic guitar](/uploads/2023/01/Ibanez-Acoustic-Guitar.jpg)
Ibanez offers comfortable necks and attractive finishes at friendly prices. **Best for:** beginners who want a bit of style.

### Washburn Acoustic-Electric
![Washburn acoustic-electric guitar](/uploads/2023/01/Washburn-Acoustic-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
Washburn's acoustic-electric models offer cutaway bodies and built-in tuners at affordable prices. **Best for:** an inexpensive plug-in option.

## How to choose

1. **Set a budget and include a setup.** A well set-up budget guitar plays better than a badly set-up expensive one.
2. **Try body sizes.** Smaller players often prefer concert or grand auditorium bodies.
3. **Decide on electronics now.** Adding a pickup later costs more.
4. **Plan for strings.** See our [best acoustic guitar strings](/best-acoustic-guitar-strings-2026).
