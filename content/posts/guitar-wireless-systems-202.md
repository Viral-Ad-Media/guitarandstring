A wireless system replaces your guitar cable with a small transmitter and receiver. It removes tripping hazards and lets you move around on stage. Modern digital systems sound clean and have very low latency. Check current models and frequency rules in your country before buying.


## What to look for

- **Latency:** the delay between playing and hearing. Under about 5 ms is hard to notice.
- **Range:** most digital systems work well within the size of a typical stage.
- **Battery life:** built-in rechargeable batteries are convenient; AA batteries are easy to swap mid-gig.
- **Receiver format:** plug-in-the-amp units are compact; pedalboard receivers are sturdier and often add a tuner.
- **Frequency band:** 2.4 GHz is license-free in most countries but can be crowded at busy venues.

## Our picks

### Shure GLX-D Guitar Pedal System
![Shure digital wireless system](/uploads/2023/01/Shure-Digital-Wireless-System.jpg)
A pedalboard receiver with automatic frequency management and a built-in tuner. **Best for:** gigging players who want a pro-level, reliable rig.

### Line 6 Relay G10
![Line 6 Relay G10](/uploads/2023/01/Line-6-Relay-G10-Digital-Guitar-Wireless-System.jpg)
The receiver plugs into your amp and charges the transmitter when docked. Extremely simple to use. **Best for:** plug-and-play convenience.

### Boss WL-50
![Boss WL-50](/uploads/2023/01/Boss-WL-50-Guitar-Wireless-System.jpg)
A pedalboard-friendly receiver that can power from your board and includes cable-tone simulation. **Best for:** pedalboard users.

### Sennheiser XSW-D Instrument Set
![Sennheiser XSW-D](/uploads/2023/01/Sennheiser-XSW-D-Wireless-System-Set.jpg)
Small, rechargeable and dead simple, with one-button pairing. **Best for:** a compact, quality-brand option.

### NUX B-5RC
![NUX B-5RC](/uploads/2023/01/NUX-B-5RC-Wireless-Guitar-System.jpg)
Budget-friendly with a charging case for both units. **Best for:** home practice and small gigs.

### Xvive U2
![Xvive U2](/uploads/2023/01/XVIVE-U2-Silver-Wireless-System-Electric-Guitar.jpg)
Tiny rechargeable plug-in units that are popular as a first wireless system. **Best for:** beginners and budget gigs.

### Samson AirLine
![Samson AirLine guitar wireless](/uploads/2023/01/Samson-Airline-88x-Guitar-Wireless-System-D-Band.jpg)
A compact system with a small transmitter that clips to the guitar. **Best for:** players who prefer a non-2.4 GHz option where available.

## Tips

1. Charge everything before the gig and keep a cable as backup.
2. Keep line of sight between transmitter and receiver when possible.
3. Don't put the receiver right next to Wi-Fi routers.
4. Some active pickups and vintage fuzz pedals behave differently with wireless. Test before the show.
