An acoustic bass lets you play bass at a campfire, in a rehearsal room or at an unplugged gig. Because bass notes need a large body to project, most acoustic basses are best used with their built-in pickup when playing with others. The models below are long-standing favorites. Check current versions and prices before buying.


## What to look for

- **Electronics:** nearly essential if you play with a drummer or a loud group.
- **Scale length:** short-scale basses are easier for guitarists and small hands; longer scales sound fuller.
- **Body size:** jumbo bodies are louder unplugged but bulkier.
- **Cutaway:** helps you reach higher frets.

## Our picks

### Taylor GS Mini-e Bass
![Taylor GS Mini-e Bass](/uploads/2023/01/Taylor-GS-Mini-e-Bass-Acoustic-Bass-Guitar.jpg)
A compact short-scale bass with special nylon-core strings that give a surprisingly deep sound. **Best for:** guitarists, travel and small hands.

### Martin BC Series
![Martin acoustic bass](/uploads/2023/01/Martin-BCE-Acoustic-Bass-Guitar.jpg)
A well-made acoustic-electric bass with Martin build quality and a warm, woody tone. **Best for:** players who want a premium feel.

### Guild Jumbo Acoustic Bass
![Guild acoustic bass](/uploads/2023/01/Guild-Guitar-Acoustic-Bass.jpg)
Guild's jumbo-bodied basses are among the loudest unplugged. **Best for:** acoustic jams without an amp.

### Fender CB-60SCE
![Fender CB-60SCE acoustic bass](/uploads/2023/01/Fender-CB-60SCE-Acoustic-Bass-Guitar.jpg)
An affordable acoustic-electric bass with a cutaway and onboard tuner. **Best for:** beginners and budget gigging.

### Fender Kingman Bass
![Fender Kingman acoustic bass](/uploads/2023/01/Fender-Kingman-Acoustic-Bass-Guitar.jpg)
A stylish acoustic-electric with a short scale and comfortable neck. **Best for:** a bass that looks as good as it sounds.

### Takamine GB72CE
![Takamine GB72CE acoustic bass](/uploads/2023/01/Takamine-GB72CE-NAT-Jumbo-Acoustic-Electric-Bass-Guitar.jpg)
A jumbo acoustic-electric bass with Takamine's reliable preamp. **Best for:** stage use.

### Cordoba Mini II Bass
![Cordoba Mini II Bass](/uploads/2023/01/Cordoba-Mini-II-Bass-EB-E-Acoustic-Electric-Bass-Guitar.jpg)
Very small and portable, using nylon-core strings for a big low end. **Best for:** travel and couch practice.

## Tips

1. Plan to plug in for anything louder than another acoustic guitar.
2. Try short-scale models if you're coming from guitar.
3. Change strings less often than on a guitar, but keep them clean.
