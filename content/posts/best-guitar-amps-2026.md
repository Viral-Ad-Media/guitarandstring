Your amp shapes your sound as much as your guitar. Today's choice runs from smart practice amps with apps to classic tube combos and digital amps that sound like vintage tube gear. These are well-regarded models across budgets. Check the current generation and price before buying.


## Tube, solid-state or modeling?

- **Tube:** classic feel and touch-sensitive breakup. Heavier and needs maintenance.
- **Solid-state:** reliable, light and usually cheaper.
- **Modeling / digital:** many amp sounds and effects in one box, often with headphone and USB recording options.

## How many watts?

For home practice, 5–40 watts is plenty, especially for solid-state or modeling amps. For gigs with a drummer, a 20–50 watt tube amp or a 50–100 watt solid-state amp usually works. Most venues also mic the amp.

## Our picks

### Boss Katana
![Boss Katana amp](/uploads/2023/01/Boss-Katana-Guitar-Amp.jpg)
Versatile, great-sounding and gig-ready, with power scaling for quiet practice. **Best for:** the best all-round value.

### Positive Grid Spark
![Positive Grid Spark amp](/uploads/2023/01/Positive-Grid-Spark-Amp.jpg)
A smart practice amp with an app full of amp models and effects, plus Bluetooth for jamming along with songs. **Best for:** home practice and learning.

### Fender Tone Master Twin Reverb
![Fender Tone Master Twin Reverb](/uploads/2023/01/Fender-Tone-Master-Twin-Reverb-Guitar-Amplifier.jpg)
A digital recreation of a legendary clean amp at a fraction of the weight, with built-in attenuation. **Best for:** big clean tones and pedal platforms.

### Marshall Origin 20
![Marshall Origin 20 combo](/uploads/2023/01/Marshall-Origin-20W-Combo-Guitar-Amp.jpg)
An affordable all-tube Marshall with classic British crunch and a power reduction switch. **Best for:** classic rock at reasonable volumes.

### Blackstar HT Series
![Blackstar HT amp](/uploads/2023/01/Blackstar-HT-Guitar-Amp.jpg)
Small tube amps with a flexible tone control that moves between British and American voices. **Best for:** tube tone at home.

### Friedman BE-Mini
![Friedman BE-Mini](/uploads/2023/01/Friedman-BE-Mini-30-Watt-Guitar-Amplifier.jpg)
A tiny 30-watt head that captures much of Friedman's high-gain character. **Best for:** rock and high-gain players who want something small.

### Hughes & Kettner TubeMeister Deluxe
![Hughes & Kettner TubeMeister Deluxe](/uploads/2023/01/Hughes-Kettner-TubeMeister-Deluxe-Guitar-Amplifier.jpg)
A compact tube amp with built-in power soaking and direct recording output. **Best for:** recording and quiet tube playing.

## Tips

1. Try your own guitar through the amp if possible.
2. Think about where you'll play most: bedroom, studio or stage.
3. A headphone output is a huge plus for practice.
