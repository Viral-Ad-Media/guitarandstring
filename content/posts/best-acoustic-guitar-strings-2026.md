The right strings can make an inexpensive acoustic sound richer and a great acoustic sound its best. Below are well-established string sets that guitarists keep coming back to, grouped by what they do best. Prices and exact product names change often, so check the current listing before you buy.

## Phosphor bronze vs. 80/20 bronze

- **Phosphor bronze:** warm, balanced, with a long-lasting tone. The most popular choice.
- **80/20 bronze:** brighter and crisper, but loses its sparkle sooner.

New to this? Read [how to choose string gauge](/how-to-choose-guitar-string-gauge) and [coated vs. uncoated strings](/coated-vs-uncoated-guitar-strings) first.

## Our picks

### Elixir Nanoweb Phosphor Bronze
![Elixir acoustic guitar strings](/uploads/2023/01/Elixir-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
Elixir made coated strings mainstream. Nanoweb sets keep a bright, natural feel while lasting far longer than uncoated strings. **Best for:** players who don't want to restring often.

### Martin Authentic Acoustic Lifespan 2.0
![Martin Lifespan acoustic strings](/uploads/2023/01/Martin-Lifespan-SP-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
Martin's treated strings resist corrosion without a thick coating, so they feel close to bare strings. **Best for:** players who dislike the slick feel of coated strings.

### Martin Authentic Acoustic SP
![Martin Authentic acoustic strings](/uploads/2023/01/Martin-Authentic-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
A classic, affordable uncoated phosphor bronze set with a warm voice. **Best for:** everyday playing and frequent restringers.

### D'Addario Phosphor Bronze
![D'Addario acoustic strings](/uploads/2023/01/D-Addario-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
D'Addario's phosphor bronze sets are a default for many players and many guitar makers. Consistent, balanced and widely available. **Best for:** a reliable benchmark set.

### Ernie Ball Earthwood
![Ernie Ball acoustic strings](/uploads/2023/01/Ernie-Ball-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
Bright, articulate and inexpensive. Great for strummers who want chords to cut through. **Best for:** rhythm players on a budget.

### Fender Dura-Tone Coated
![Fender Dura-Tone acoustic strings](/uploads/2023/01/Fender-Dura-Tone-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
A coated 80/20 set that offers longer life at a friendlier price than premium coated brands. **Best for:** trying coated strings without paying top price.

### Dean Markley Blue Steel
![Dean Markley acoustic strings](/uploads/2023/01/Dean-Markley-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
Cryogenically treated for durability, with a bright, punchy tone. **Best for:** players who like a lively sound.

### Gibson Masterbuilt Premium
![Gibson acoustic strings](/uploads/2023/01/Gibson-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
Phosphor bronze strings with a rich, full tone that pairs well with dreadnoughts and jumbos. **Best for:** big-bodied acoustics.

### Curt Mangan Phosphor Bronze
![Curt Mangan acoustic strings](/uploads/2023/01/Curt-Mangan-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
A smaller maker with a loyal following for its clear, even tone and consistency. **Best for:** players who want to try something beyond the big brands.

### Rotosound Tru Bronze
![Rotosound Tru Bronze strings](/uploads/2023/01/Rotosound-Tru-Bronze-Acoustic-Guitar-String.jpg)
A bright, British-made 80/20-style set with plenty of sparkle. **Best for:** recording and fingerstyle players who want clarity.

## How to choose

1. **Pick your alloy:** phosphor bronze for warmth, 80/20 for brightness.
2. **Pick your gauge:** light (12–53) is the safe default.
3. **Decide on coating:** coated if you restring rarely, uncoated if you change often.
4. **Experiment:** strings are cheap compared to guitars. Try a few sets and keep notes.
