Guitar and String is a site for guitar players of every level. We write practical guides on choosing guitars, strings, capos, amps and accessories, plus how-tos for everyday maintenance like changing strings.

## How we choose products

Our recommendation lists focus on well-established products with strong reputations among players. Each pick explains who it's best for, so you can match it to your own playing. Prices and model versions change often, so always check the current listing before you buy.

## Affiliate links

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes which products we recommend.
